The playbook’s point of view

This is not an AI build guide. It is an enterprise delivery guide.

The strongest lesson from CRA is that success sits at the intersection of business process, data contracts, deterministic controls, secure integrations, governance, testing and adoption.

01 · Frame

Start with a decision, not a model

Define what the agent does, recommends and must never decide.

02 · Ground

Make data a product

Align semantics, ownership, freshness, null treatment and merge keys.

03 · Control

Separate reasoning from calculation

Keep financial logic deterministic, testable and auditable.

04 · Prove

Design for failure before scale

Use fallbacks, human gates, golden tests and immutable evidence.
